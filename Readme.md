# README: Do.Ze Premium Newsletter Campaign

## 1. Project Overview
*   **Brand:** Do.Ze (Premium Ladies Gym Wear)
*   **Project:** "Earth & Motion" Collection Launch Newsletter
*   **Objective:** To showcase the new range of high-end jumpsuits and gym sets using an elegant, high-fashion aesthetic.

---

## 2. Visual Identity & Aesthetic
The design must follow a **"Quiet Luxury"** and **"Minimalist"** style. Avoid "loud" sales graphics or neon colors.

### **Color Palette (Hex Codes for Agent)**
*   **Cream White:** `#F9F8F4` (Primary Background)
*   **Nude/Sand:** `#DBCBB0` (Accents & Boxes)
*   **Mocha Brown:** `#4B3621` (Headings & Primary CTA)
*   **Deep Espresso:** `#2C1E12` (Main Body Text)

### **Typography**
*   **Headlines:** High-contrast Serif (e.g., *Playfair Display* or *Bodoni*).
*   **Body:** Clean Sans-Serif (e.g., *Montserrat Light* or *Inter*).

---

## 3. Content Structure
The newsletter should be divided into the following sections:

1.  **Hero Section:** High-resolution image of the signature jumpsuit with the Do.Ze logo overlay.
2.  **The Story:** A brief, 2-sentence intro about the "Earth & Motion" collection.
3.  **Product Grid:** 4-square grid showcasing different sets (Leggings, Bras, Jumpsuits).
4.  **Key Benefits:** Minimalist icons for "Ribbed Fabric," "Seamless Design," and "High-Impact Support."
5.  **CTA (Call to Action):** A single, centered "Shop the Collection" button.
6.  **Social Footer:** Instagram handle and minimalist icons for website and email.

---

## 4. Image Requirements
*   **Vibe:** Neutral, warm-toned, and natural lighting.
*   **Formatting:** All images must have a slight warm/sepia filter to maintain color consistency across the "Nude/Brown" palette.
*   **Ratio:** Use a mix of 4:5 (portrait) for hero shots and 1:1 (square) for the product grid.

---

## 5. Technical Delivery
*   **Platform:** Compatible with Mailchimp / Klaviyo / Beehiiv.
*   **Responsiveness:** Must be mobile-first (optimized for iPhone/Android screens).
*   **File Format:** Source file (Figma/Canva) + exported HTML or High-Res PNG version.

---

## 6. Tone of Voice
*   **Sophisticated:** Use words like *Sculpt, Timeless, Effortless, and Motion.*
*   **Exclusive:** The copy should make the reader feel like they are part of a private club of elite athletes/fashionistas.
